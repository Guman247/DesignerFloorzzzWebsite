Garage Floor Coatings

Transform your garage into a showroom

Professional Garage Floor Coating Services

Your garage floor takes a beating every day - from vehicle traffic and oil drips to chemical spills and heavy equipment. At Designer Floorzzz, we specialize in transforming worn, stained concrete into beautiful, durable surfaces that stand up to anything you throw at them.

Why Choose Epoxy for Your Garage?

  • ✓ Resists oil, gas, and chemical stains
  • ✓ Easy to clean and maintain
  • ✓ Increases light reflection by up to 300%
  • ✓ Hides imperfections in concrete
  • ✓ Available in countless colors and styles
  • ✓ Lasts 15-20+ years with proper care

Our Process

We start with thorough surface preparation, including diamond grinding and crack repair. Then we apply a premium epoxy base coat followed by your choice of decorative flakes or metallic pigments. Finally, we seal everything with a high-performance topcoat for maximum durability.
